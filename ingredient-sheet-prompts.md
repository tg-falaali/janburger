# Ingredient sheet prompts

The two prompts that produced the photo-real ingredient sheets. Settings used:
portrait 2:3, transparent background, about 1536 x 2304 px.

Each sheet is a grid of 2 columns x 5 rows. Keep that layout if you generate
them again with another tool - it is what lets the sheet be cut into separate
layers.

## Sheet 1 - burger ingredients

```text
Photorealistic food photography asset sheet on a fully transparent background. Ten separate hamburger ingredients, each one isolated and floating on its own, arranged in a tidy grid of 2 columns and 5 rows with wide empty transparent gaps between every item. Nothing touches or overlaps anything else. Every item is photographed from the same angle: a low three-quarter side view, slightly from above, like the layers of an exploded burger advertisement. Bright, even, soft studio lighting, crisp detail, appetising commercial quality. Every item is roughly the same width and fills its grid cell.

Grid, reading left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: (left) top half of a golden sesame seed burger bun, domed, flat cut underside; (right) bottom half of a burger bun with its toasted golden cut face showing on top.
Row 2: (left) a single ruffled leaf of fresh green lettuce lying flat, wide and round; (right) three raw red onion rings lying flat in a loose overlapping cluster.
Row 3: (left) one thick round slice of fresh red tomato lying flat; (right) one square slice of melted yellow cheddar cheese lying flat with softly drooping corners.
Row 4: (left) one round grilled beef burger patty with dark charcoal grill marks, juicy; (right) one round crispy golden fried chicken burger fillet with breadcrumb crust.
Row 5: (left) one flat fried egg with a glossy round yellow yolk, seen from the same low side angle; (right) four round green pickle slices lying flat in a row.

No plate, no table, no shadows on the background, no text, no labels, no numbers, no borders, no grid lines, no hands, no extra items.
```

## Sheet 2 - hot dog, sauces, fries, drinks

```text
Photorealistic food photography asset sheet on a fully transparent background. Ten separate fast-food items, each one isolated and floating on its own, arranged in a tidy grid of 2 columns and 5 rows with wide empty transparent gaps between every item. Nothing touches or overlaps anything else. Every item is photographed from the same angle: a low three-quarter side view, slightly from above, like the layers of an exploded burger advertisement. Bright, even, soft studio lighting, crisp detail, appetising commercial quality. Each item fills its grid cell.

Grid, reading left to right, top to bottom:
Row 1: (left) the top half of a long golden hot dog bun, plain with no seeds, domed, flat cut underside; (right) the bottom half of a long hot dog bun with its pale soft cut face showing on top.
Row 2: (left) one long grilled hot dog sausage with light grill marks, lying horizontally; (right) one long wavy zigzag drizzle of red ketchup lying flat, horizontal, glossy.
Row 3: (left) a round flat glossy splash of red ketchup sauce, the size of a burger; (right) a round flat dollop of creamy white mayonnaise sauce, the size of a burger.
Row 4: (left) a loose upright bunch of golden french fries with no container; (right) an empty plain red paper french fries carton, front view, no logo.
Row 5: (left) a small flat glossy puddle of melted yellow cheese sauce; (right) a white paper soft drink cup with a red band, a clear plastic dome lid and a red straw, no logo, standing next to a small clear plastic water bottle with a blue cap and a plain blue label, the two clearly separated with a gap between them.

No plate, no table, no shadows on the background, no text, no labels, no brand names, no numbers, no borders, no grid lines, no hands, no extra items.
```

## Using the result

Save the two images as `sheet-1.png` and `sheet-2.png` in this folder. They are
then cut into one transparent PNG per ingredient and used as the menu's layers.
